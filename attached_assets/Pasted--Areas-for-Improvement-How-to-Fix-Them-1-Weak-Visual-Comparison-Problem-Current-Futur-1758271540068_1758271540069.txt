🎯 Areas for Improvement & How to Fix Them:

1. Weak Visual Comparison:

· Problem: "Current | Future" is too abstract. A simple text comparison doesn't feel inspiring or real.
· Solution: Use visual avatars. Even simple silhouette illustrations would be 100x more powerful.
  · Current: A slimmer silhouette labeled "67.4kg".
  · Future: A more muscular silhouette labeled "79.9kg".
  · This single change would make the screen dramatically more engaging.

2. Boring Data Visualization:

· Problem: The stats in the "Analysis" box are just a list. It doesn't feel like a personalized plan.
· Solution:
  · Turn "+12.5kg" into a mini progress bar filling from left to right.
  · Turn "25w" into a timeline graphic, like a line with notches for key phases.
  · Make the "Status: Normal" tag a colored pill (e.g., a soft blue).

3. Wasted Space & Layout:

· Problem: The right side of the screen is empty, while the left is crowded. The balance is off.
· Solution: Rearrange the elements. The weight comparison (with avatars) should be the hero at the top. The "Analysis" and "Weight Progress" boxes could sit side-by-side underneath on larger screens, or stack neatly on mobile.

4. Passive Language:

· Problem: "Your starting point" feels like an ending. This screen should launch the user into their journey.
· Solution: Use active, motivational language.
  · Instead of: "Your starting point"
  · Try: "Your transformation starts now!" or "Ready to begin your journey?"

5. Missing Call to Action (CTA):

· Problem: The "See My Future →" button is good, but it's not the main action. What does the user do now?
· Solution: The primary button on this screen should be something like "Start My Plan" or "Begin Journey". This gives the user a clear next step and creates momentum.
