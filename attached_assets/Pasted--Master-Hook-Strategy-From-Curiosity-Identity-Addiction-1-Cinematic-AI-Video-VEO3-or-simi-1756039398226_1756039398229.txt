🔥 Master Hook Strategy: “From Curiosity → Identity → Addiction”
1. Cinematic AI Video (VEO3 or similar)

Opening scene: futuristic lab, holograms, your AI coach appears.

Script idea:
“Welcome, hardgainer. You’re about to use technology from the year 2125 to do what nature never intended… gain weight fast. No more failed diets. No more endless struggle. I’ll guide you, personally. Your future body is waiting.”

Show a quick transformation preview of a generic hardgainer body → bulked version.

End with:
“Press start. Your calibration begins now. Every day you log, I’m calculating the exact formula for YOUR muscle growth.”

👉 This gives goosebumps and frames calibration as part of the magic, not a chore.

2. Onboarding Gamification

Instead of boring data entry, we make it feel like a mission:

Day 0 (“The Initiation”):

User uploads weight, food, and activity → AI “unlocks” their baseline.

At the end:
“Calibration 1/7 complete. Your body is syncing with the protocol.”

Reward: a futuristic holographic bar fills up (like syncing DNA).

Day 3 checkpoint:
AI shows: “Already I’ve learned 43% of your metabolism. That’s why other people quit… but not you. Stay on course.”

Day 7 (“Unlock moment”):

AI announces: “Calibration complete. Your metabolic blueprint is unlocked.”

App reveals first custom meal plan, workout, and body projection.

🎉 Confetti animation, premium feel.

3. Social Proof / Accountability

Add mini-sharing moments in onboarding:

“Share your futuristic projection with a friend” → auto-generated before/after mockup (even if just rough).

Creates virality while user feels proud.

4. Daily Micro-Hooks

Every log-in = reward.

AI says one line of futuristic encouragement (not generic).

Example:

“Aron, your protein intake is 72% of target. Adjust this today, and you accelerate muscle gain by 13%.”

Keeps it science-based + personal.

5. Premium Experience

Don’t feel like a free app. Make it feel like secret access to technology from the future.

Dark sci-fi design.

Smooth futuristic sound effects.

Polished AI video as centerpiece.

Limit friction, everything flows naturally.
