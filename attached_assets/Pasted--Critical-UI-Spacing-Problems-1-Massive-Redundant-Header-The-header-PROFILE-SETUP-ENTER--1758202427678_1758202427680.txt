🔴 Critical UI/Spacing Problems:
1. Massive, Redundant Header:

The header PROFILE SETUP / ENTER YOUR BODY DATA is enormous and repeats the same information.

The Fremdrift20% COMPLETE bar is unnecessarily tall and could be integrated more sleekly.

2. Wasted Vertical Space:

There is a huge amount of empty space between the header, the progress bar, the "Alt klart" message, and the actual form fields. This forces the user to scroll for no reason.

The "Back" and "Next" buttons are floating far below the content, adding to the empty feel.

3. Repetitive and Unnecessary Content:

The text ● Alt klart for din plan! Tell us about your body... is shown on every single screen of the onboarding. It wastes valuable vertical real estate once the user has read it the first time.

4. Weak Information Hierarchy:

The form labels (FIRST NAME, AGE) are too small and lack visual weight.

The placeholder text [Hva heter du?] is visually confusing with brackets and doesn't look like a tappable field.
