🖼 Wireframe Layout: Free vs Premium

⸻

Onboarding Flow

Screen 1 – Welcome
	•	Full-screen dark background (gradient like Grok).
	•	Centered Title: “Welcome to the Future of Fitness”
	•	Subtitle: “We make the impossible possible for hardgainers.”
	•	CTA Button: “Start My Transformation” (large, primary color).

⸻

Screen 2 – User Info
	•	Progress bar at top (Step 1 of 5).
	•	Input fields stacked vertically: Age, Gender, Height, Current Weight, Goal Weight.
	•	Friendly text under each field: “Your journey starts here…”
	•	Bottom: “Next” button.

⸻

Screen 3 – Current Body Preview
	•	Placeholder 3D avatar in center (expandable later).
	•	Title above: “Here’s You Today.”
	•	Subtitle below: “This is your digital twin. Together, we’ll evolve it week by week.”
	•	Bottom CTA: “See My Future.”

⸻

Screen 4 – Future Body Preview
	•	Split screen: Current Body (left, faded) vs Future Body (right, highlighted).
	•	Title: “The Future You.”
	•	Free Tier: blurred/faded preview.
	•	Premium: realistic 3D preview with muscles.
	•	Two buttons:
	•	“I Want This” (continues to calibration).
	•	“Unlock Premium Features” (opens upsell).

⸻

Screen 5 – Calibration Intro
	•	Simple checklist UI:
	•	✅ Log meals
	•	✅ Log weight
	•	✅ Log training
	•	Title: “Your Body, My Science.”
	•	Subtitle: “For 7 days, I’ll measure your metabolism and design your surplus.”
	•	CTA: “Begin Calibration.”

⸻

⸻

Premium Upsell Screen

Header: Full-width banner “Unlock Your Full Potential”

Section 1: Feature Grid (icons + short text)
	•	🍲 AI Meal Plans
	•	🛒 Grocery Lists
	•	🏋 Progressive Training
	•	🧬 Monthly Recalibration
	•	🏆 Premium Badges
	•	🧍 3D Future Body

Section 2: Pricing Cards
	•	Monthly: $6.99 (highlighted in gray).
	•	Yearly: $59.99 (highlighted as “Most Popular”).
	•	Lifetime: $99 (gold border, labeled “Founder’s Offer”).

Section 3: CTA Buttons
	•	Primary: “Upgrade Now and Transform Faster” (glowing, large).
	•	Secondary: “Maybe Later” (small, muted).

⸻

Gamification Layer (Home Screen)
	•	Daily Routine Checklist (cards you can check off).
	•	XP bar at top → Level system.
	•	Streak counter → flame icon.
	•	Badge carousel → locked badges grayed out.

Premium users: extra badges + AI challenges show up.

⸻

⚡ With this wireframe:
	•	Replit devs know exactly where each element goes.
	•	Free vs Premium split is obvious but motivating.
	•	Onboarding flows directly into calibration + upsell without boring drop-offs.
